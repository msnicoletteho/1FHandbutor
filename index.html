Card 1 of 4
Super Word 1
and

Joining similar or extra ideas!

We use and to put two happy or similar things together.

Example 1

I like apples and bananas.

Example 2

Tom ran and jumped high.

Super Word 2
or

Showing choices or options!

We use or when we have to choose between one thing or another.

Example 1

Would you like milk or juice?

Example 2

Can we read a book or draw?

Super Word 3
but

Showing a different or surprising idea!

We use but to join two opposite or contrasting thoughts.

Example 1

I wanted to play outside, but it rained.

Example 2

The dog is small, but very fast!
